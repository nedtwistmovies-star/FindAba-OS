### Carry-Go WhatsApp Flow Templates

#### 1. Start Conversation (Sender)
**English:**  
"Welcome to Carry-Go 🚚. I am your logistics bot for Aba.  
Reply 1 to Send a Parcel  
Reply 2 to Track a Shipment  
Reply 3 to Support"  

**Pidgin:**  
"Welcome to Carry-Go 🚚. I go help you carry your load for Aba.  
Type 1 if you want Send Load  
Type 2 to see where your load dey  
Type 3 for Help"  

#### 2. Booking - Pickup Location
**English:**  
"Please send your pickup address or share your location 📍.  
Example: 'Ariaria Gate 3, beside blue kiosk'."  

**Pidgin:**  
"Abeg send where we go pick the load or share your location 📍.  
Example: 'Ariaria Gate 3, beside blue kiosk'."  

#### 3. Booking - Quote
**English:**  
"Quote for {size} parcel to {destination}:  
Distance: {distance}km  
Total: ₦{amount}  
Rider Payout: ₦{payout}  
Platform Fee: ₦{fee}  
Reply 1 to Pay & Book."  

**Pidgin:**  
"Price to carry {size} load go {destination}:  
Distance: {distance}km  
Total: ₦{amount}  
Reply 1 make you Pay & Book."  

#### 4. Payment Confirmed (Sender)
**English:**  
"Payment confirmed ✅. Your rider {carrier_name} ({carrier_phone}) has been assigned.  
Track live: {tracking_url}"  

**Pidgin:**  
"Payment don enter ✅. Your rider name na {carrier_name} ({carrier_phone}).  
Follow am for here: {tracking_url}"  

#### 5. Delivery Confirmation (Sender)
**English:**  
"Your parcel {tracking_id} has been delivered 📦.  
Proof: {photo_url}  
Reply 1 to CONFIRM receipt and release funds to rider.  
Reply 2 if there is an issue."  

**Pidgin:**  
"Your load {tracking_id} don reach 📦.  
See the photo: {photo_url}  
Type 1 if you don collect am so we go pay rider.  
Type 2 if anything happen to your load."  

#### 6. Payout Confirmed (Carrier)
**English:**  
"₦{amount} has been sent to your {bank} account for delivery {tracking_id}. Well done! 🚀"  

**Pidgin:**  
"₦{amount} don land for your {bank} account for the load {tracking_id}. You try! 🚀"
