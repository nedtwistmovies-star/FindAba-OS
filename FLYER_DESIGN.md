## Flyer Content Strategy

### 1. Headline & Hero
- **Text:** FindAba: Connecting Aba to the World.
- **Visual:** FindAba Logo (A stylized 'A' with network nodes).

### 2. Why Join FindAba?
- **Verified Registry:** Only authentic Aba businesses.
- **Market Insights:** Real-time updates on market days and news.
- **Global Reach:** Take your products beyond the city limits.

### 3. How to Get Started (Self-Onboarding)
1. **Visit:** Open [findaba.com.ng](https://findaba.com.ng) on your browser.
2. **Register:** Click on 'Sign Up' and choose 'Business Owner' or 'Visitor'.
3. **Set Up:** Fill in your details and verify your phone number.
4. **Explore:** Start listing your products or searching for vendors.

### 4. Color Palette
- **Primary:** Deep Space Blue (`#020617`)
- **Secondary:** Royal Purple
- **Accent:** White / Light Gray
